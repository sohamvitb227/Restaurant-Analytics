Key Insights

  • The dataset contains 9,551 restaurant records.
  • North Indian cuisine has the highest restaurant count among the analysed cuisines.
  • Chinese cuisine is also among the most widely represented cuisines.
  • Asian cuisine has the highest average rating among the top 10 cuisines by total votes.
  • Restaurant ratings and votes are useful variables for exploring customer engagement and preferences.
  • Random Forest performed best among the three tested regression models in the current evaluation.
